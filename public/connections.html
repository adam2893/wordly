How to Play

Find groups of four words that share something in common.

  • Tap four words, then Submit to check whether they're a group.
  • Find all four groups without making 4 mistakes.
  • "One away…" means three of your four words belong together.

Categories are more specific than "5-letter words" or "names". Watch out for words that seem to fit more than one group — each puzzle has exactly one solution.

Daily gives everyone the same puzzle, resetting at midnight, and counts on the leaderboard: fewer mistakes is better, and time breaks ties. The clock only runs while the game is open on screen. Practice gives you any puzzle from the archive.
