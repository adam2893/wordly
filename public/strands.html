How to Play Strandy

Uncover all hidden theme words and the Spangram to fill every letter of the 6×8 board.

  • Theme words (blue) relate to Today's Theme clue.
  • The Spangram (gold) describes the theme and touches two opposite sides of the board.
  • Drag your finger across adjacent letters (up, down, left, right, or diagonal), or tap letters one by one and tap the last letter again (or Submit).
  • No theme words overlap, and every letter on the board is used once.
  • Need a nudge? Tap Hint 💡 once to highlight a theme word's letters, and a second time to reveal its letter order.
