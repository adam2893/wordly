How to Play

Match overlapping visual layers across tiles until the entire 5×6 board is cleared.

  • Each tile has up to 3 layers (outer shape, middle motif, inner center).
  • Tap a tile, then tap a second tile that shares at least one matching layer.
  • Every shared layer disappears from both tiles, and your Combo increases by 1.
  • The second tile stays selected so you can chain matches into a continuous run!
  • If your selected tile becomes completely empty, you can hop to any tile on the board without breaking your combo.

Clear all 30 tiles with 0 misses to achieve a Perfect 45/45 combo!
