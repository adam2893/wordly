      How to Play

      Fill every white square in the daily full-sized crossword.

      • Tap a square to select it. Tap the active square (or the clue bar) to switch between Across and Down.
      • Use ‹ and › to jump between clues.
      • Check word, Reveal letter, and Reveal word are there if you get stuck — solving with 0 hints earns a Flawless ★ badge on the leaderboard.

      Daily gives everyone the same puzzle, resetting at midnight, and counts on the leaderboard: fewest hints wins, and fastest time breaks ties. The clock only runs while the page is open. Practice gives you any puzzle from the archive.

      Keyboard: Arrows move, Space flips direction, Tab / Enter next clue.
