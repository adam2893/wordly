How to Play Searchy

Find every hidden theme word in the grid.

  • Words run in straight lines: horizontally, vertically, or diagonally (forwards or backwards).
  • Drag your finger from the first letter to the last letter of a word, or tap the first letter and then tap the last letter.
  • Choose Easy (8×8), Medium (10×10), or Hard (12×12) in the header — each has its own daily puzzle and leaderboard.
  • Use Hint 💡 to highlight the starting letter of an unfound word. Solving with 0 hints earns a Flawless ★ badge!
